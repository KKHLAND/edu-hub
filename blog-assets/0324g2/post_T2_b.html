W: Then what's the reason? Your leg is fine now, right?
M: Yes, it's completely healed. The thing is, my family is moving to Busan next month because of my mom's new job.
W: Oh, I didn't know that. I'll really miss playing with you.
M: Me too. Let's keep in touch.

W: Sure. Let me see the list. How much do you want to spend?
M: I don't want to spend more than thirty-five dollars.
W: Okay. What about the battery capacity? This one only has five thousand milliamp hours.
M: That's too small. I need at least ten thousand, so I can charge my phone twice.
W: Good thinking. You'll carry it in your school bag every day, so the weight matters, too.
M: Right. I want one under two hundred fifty grams.
W: Then you have two options left. Do you need wireless charging?
M: Not really. My phone doesn't support it anyway. I'll take the lighter one.
W: Okay. Let's order it now.

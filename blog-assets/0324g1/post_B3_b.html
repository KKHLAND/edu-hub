2. 쌤이 만든 유사 독해 문제

유사주어진 글 다음에 이어질 글의 순서로 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.순서 (36번 유형)

When people want to remember something important, they often write it down on a piece of paper.

(A) Because of this, some researchers suggest a simple strategy: write things down, but then try to recall them without looking. The note should be a safety net, not a replacement for memory.

(B) This habit seems wise, since paper does not forget. However, studies show that when we know information is stored somewhere else, our brain puts less effort into keeping it.

(C) For example, in one experiment, participants who were told their notes would be saved remembered fewer details than those who were told their notes would be deleted. They had relied on the paper instead of their own memory.

  1. ① (A) - (C) - (B)
  2. ② (B) - (A) - (C)
  3. ③ (B) - (C) - (A)
  4. ④ (C) - (A) - (B)
  5. ⑤ (C) - (B) - (A)
정답과 해설 보기

정답 ③

(B)의 This habit이 주어진 글의 '종이에 적어 두는 습관'을 받고, However로 '뇌가 덜 노력한다'는 주장을 꺼냅니다. (C)는 For example로 그 주장을 실험으로 보여 주고, (A)의 Because of this가 실험 결과를 받아 해결책을 제시합니다. 기출 36번처럼 지시어(This habit)와 연결어(For example, Because of this)가 순서를 정해 줍니다.
